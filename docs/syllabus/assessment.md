# Assessment & Grading

## Assessment Table

| Assessment | Weight | Week | CLOs |
|---|---|---|---|
| Quizzes | 10% | 3 | CLO 1, 2 |
| Test | 15% | 7 | CLO 1, 2, 3 |
| Individual Assignment | 10% | 10 | CLO 3, 4, 5 |
| Group Assignment | 15% | 10 | CLO 2, 3 |
| Final Exam | 50% | 15 | CLO 1–6 |
| **Total** | **100%** | | |

The CLOs are defined on the [Learning Outcomes](/syllabus/learning-outcomes) page.

Continuous assessment is the Quizzes, Test, Individual Assignment and Group Assignment (50% in total).

## Pass Requirements

To pass the course a student must:

- attempt **all** assessments;
- score at least **40%** in the final exam; **and**
- score at least **50%** in continuous assessment.

The final exam and continuous assessment thresholds are applied **independently**.

## Late Submission

Late submissions lose **10% of the mark for every 24 hours** of delay.

## Attendance

- Lectures: at least **80%** attendance.
- Practicals: **100%** attendance.

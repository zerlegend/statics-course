# Frequently Asked Questions

## Assessment and rules

**What do I need to pass?**
You must attempt all assessments, score at least 40% in the final exam, and score at least 50% in continuous assessment. The two thresholds are applied independently. See [Assessment & Grading](/syllabus/assessment).

**What happens if I submit late?**
You lose 10% of the mark for every 24 hours of delay.

**What attendance is required?**
At least 80% of lectures and 100% of practicals.

**How are the CLOs assessed?**
See the table on the [Learning Outcomes](/syllabus/learning-outcomes) page.

## Using the site

**Which textbook should I use?**
The primary textbook is Meriam & Kraige, *Engineering Mechanics: Statics* (SI), 7th Ed. Each chapter page has a Textbook Mapping box, and the [Reading Guide](/resources/reading-guide) gives page numbers.

**Why does the course chapter number differ from the textbook chapter number?**
Area moments of inertia (course Chapter 6) are in Appendix A of the textbook, and friction (course Chapter 7) is textbook Chapter 6.

**When do I see the full solutions?**
Full worked solutions are released by the instructor as the course progresses. Hidden answers in each chapter are always available.

**The textbook has topics that are not on the site. Do I need them?**
Beams, flexible cables, fluid statics and virtual work are outside the course CLOs. You may read them for interest.

**How do I cite this site or the textbook?**
Use the referencing style required by your assignment (IEEE, ASME or APA). See [Policies](/syllabus/policies).

# Start Here

Welcome to **Engineering Mechanics I (CEng2021)**. This page explains how to get the most from the course site in ten minutes.

## Your weekly routine

1. **Check the [Weekly Schedule](/syllabus/schedule)** to see this week's chapter, reading and any deadline.
2. **Read the chapter before the lecture.** Start with the learning objectives and the *Real-World Application* box, then skim the key concepts.
3. **Read the matching textbook sections.** The [Reading Guide](/resources/reading-guide) gives the page numbers in Meriam & Kraige, 7th Ed.
4. **Work the examples on paper** before reading the solutions.
5. **Attempt the tutorial problems.** Open the hidden answer only after a genuine attempt, then compare with the full solution once it is released.
6. **Finish with the Self-Check and the Quick Quiz** at the bottom of the chapter.

## Good habits for statics

- Always draw a **free-body diagram** and choose axes before writing any equation.
- Write **units** on every number and check that equations are dimensionally consistent.
- **Check your answer**: use a second equation, or ask whether the sign and size make sense.
- Keep the [Formula Sheet](/resources/formula-sheet) next to you, but understand where each formula comes from.

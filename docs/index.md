# Engineering Mechanics I (Statics)

**Course Code:** CEng2021 · **Year II / Semester I** · **Academic Year 2026/2027**
Department of Civil Engineering, College of Engineering

## Course Overview

Engineering Mechanics I introduces the fundamental principles of statics: how forces act on bodies that are at rest. Starting from scalars and vectors, the course develops force systems in two and three dimensions, equilibrium of particles and rigid bodies, analysis of simple structures (trusses, frames and machines), centroids, area moments of inertia, and dry friction. These tools underpin later courses in structural analysis, strength of materials and design.

# Textbooks

Links below go to publisher or general library pages only. Please obtain the books through the library, the bookstore or the publisher. Do not share or download unauthorized copies.

## Primary Textbook {#meriam}

**Meriam, J.L. and Kraige, L.G.**, *Engineering Mechanics: Statics* (SI version), 7th Edition, John Wiley & Sons.

Publisher: [Wiley](https://www.wiley.com) (search for the title or author).

### Course chapter to Meriam 7th Ed. mapping

| Course chapter | Meriam 7th Ed. |
|---|---|
| 1. Scalars and Vectors | Chapter 1 |
| 2. Force Systems | Chapter 2 (2D and 3D force systems) |
| 3. Equilibrium | Chapter 3 |
| 4. Analysis of Simple Structures | Chapter 4 (trusses, frames, machines) |
| 5. Centroids | Chapter 5, Sections 5/2–5/5 only |
| 6. Area Moments of Inertia | **Appendix A** (A/1–A/4) |
| 7. Friction | **Chapter 6** (offset: course Ch7 = Meriam Ch6) |

::: tip Section titles
Section numbers and titles above follow the 7th Edition table of contents. Page numbers can differ between printings, so use the section numbers when looking things up.
:::
